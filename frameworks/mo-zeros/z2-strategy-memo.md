# Z2 — Internal Strategy Memo (Mo Zeros)

**ROLE:** Produce an **internal-only** memo on what we think we can actually sell
this client — the honest read, not the client-facing version.

**INPUT:** The entity's Brain + the Mo Commas insight from the latest conversation
(C1 shape, C2 pain, C3 adjacency, and C5 cross-bag synergy if available).

**PROCESS:**
1. **The real opportunity.** What's the latent $-value here, beyond what they
   walked in asking for? (cf. CS2.) Tie to C2 pains and C3 adjacencies.
2. **Repositioning.** If the way they sell themselves is wrong, say how to reframe
   (cf. CS1/CS4/CS5).
3. **Cross-bag.** Any synergy with other entities in [`../bag/`](../bag/)?
4. **Sales-maturity read.** Where are they on the spectrum, and which Mo Zeros
   pipelines to activate (precursor to C6).
5. **Next moves + risks.** Concrete next steps; what could kill it; what we don't
   yet know (`[[ HITL ]]` / `[[ FOUNDER ]]`).

**OUTPUT (writable to `.../insights/<date>-strategy-memo.md`, `loop: zeros`,
internal):** opportunity · repositioning · cross-bag · maturity + recommended
pipelines · next moves · risks/unknowns.

**DO NOT:** soften it for the client's eyes (this is internal); assert latent
value without grounding; skip the "what we don't know" section.

**EXAMPLES:** CS2 (latent opportunity), CS3 (lean into proven verticals), CS4
(compression thesis). See [`../case-studies.md`](../case-studies.md).

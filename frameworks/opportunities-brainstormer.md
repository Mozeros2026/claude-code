# Opportunities Brainstormer (C13) — Mo Commas

> Lifted from OPPORTUNITIES BRAINSTORMER v1. Pure "what if" mode: take what a
> client has (product, assets, team, relationships, IP) and speculate sideways —
> how else it could be packaged, sold, or deployed to different buyers, markets,
> or channels. Generates angles; does not evaluate or filter (that's Mo's job).
> The generative cousin of the Adjacent Market Mapper (C4). Build-OS stripped →
> `/momega-opportunities`.

## Input
Asymmetry Detector report (C12) + Brain (if available) + optional cross-portfolio
context + optional market signals. Output → `entities/<type>/<id>/insights/<date>-opportunities.md`.

## Output format (exact)

```
OPPORTUNITIES BRAINSTORM · CLIENT: <name> | DATE: <date>
HYPOTHETICAL ANGLES: <count>

ANGLE #N: [HEADLINE]
THESIS: [what if we sold/positioned/packaged this as X to buyer Y?]
WHAT THEY HAVE THAT ENABLES THIS: [assets / relationship / capability]
WHO COULD BUY THIS: [segment — what they'd value]
GO-TO-MARKET HYPOTHESIS: [how to reach them; inbound signal; champion]
POSITIONING FRAME: [how to make it sound natural, not like a pivot]
POTENTIAL REVENUE SCALE: [order of magnitude — $X/txn, Y customers/yr]
PREREQUISITE / FIRST QUESTION: [what to know/build to test it]

PATTERN SUMMARY: [if 3+: the common thread]
HIGHEST-LEVERAGE ANGLE: [which, if validated, has biggest impact — why]
```

## Angle types to hunt
1. Same product, different market/buyer · 2. Same market, different value
prop/positioning · 3. Byproduct / data / insight as revenue · 4. Embedded
operator / implementation as service revenue · 5. Licensing / platform /
underlying tech · 6. Adjacent market via existing relationships · 7.
Timing/trigger-based upsell · 8. International/geographic · 9. Founder/team as
asset · 10. Inverse/competitive.

## Core rules
- **Speculative OK, hallucination not** — every angle grounded in something they
  actually have; never invent assets.
- **Stay true to what they have** — reframe their real product into the new
  market, don't pretend they have capabilities they lack.
- **No "bad idea" filtering** — brainstorm it, let Mo decide; the best ones sound
  silly first.
- **Revenue is rough** — order of magnitude, not unit economics.
- **Cross-portfolio is a gift** — note "seen at X" as signal, but ground in *this*
  client's assets; don't transfer angles.

## Relationship
Runs parallel to Brain Builder + Asymmetry Detector; best paired with C12 (weak
asymmetry input → noisy angles). Its angles feed the opportunity-selection
checkpoint, then the **Proposal Generator** (C14). Reconcile-on-use with C4
Adjacent Market Mapper (this is the broader speculative superset).

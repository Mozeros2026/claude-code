# C2 — Pain Hypothesizer (Mo Commas)

**ROLE:** From a buyer conversation, produce ranked hypotheses about what the
buyer *actually pays to solve* — in the buyer's own language — framed so each can
be tested in the next meeting.

**INPUT:** Buyer/discovery transcript + the entity's Brain export (§2 people, §4
customers, §10 asymmetries).

**PROCESS:**
1. **Separate stated from latent.** What they said they want vs what their
   behavior/spend implies (cf. CS2: stated "bodies," latent $50–100M elsewhere).
2. **Translate into buyer language.** Phrase each pain the way *they* would say
   it, not in our jargon (cf. CS5: same product, two buyer-language frames).
3. **Rank by willingness-to-pay × urgency.** What would they actually cut a check
   for, soon?
4. **Apply the test framing.** For each: *"If I solved X, would that be worth
   another meeting?"* — that's the falsifiable hook.
5. **Flag missing signal.** Where you're guessing at psychology, mark
   `[[ FOUNDER ]]` / `[[ HITL ]]` rather than asserting.

**OUTPUT (writable to `.../insights/<date>-pain.md`):** ranked list — *pain (buyer
words) · why we believe it (evidence/Brain cite) · WTP/urgency · "worth another
meeting?" test · confidence*.

**DO NOT:** confabulate buyer psychology; default to generic B2B pains; present a
guess as a finding. Low signal → say so.

**EXAMPLES:** CS2 (latent-opportunity extraction), CS5 (dual-language framing).
See [`../case-studies.md`](../case-studies.md).

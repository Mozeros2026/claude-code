# C4 — Adjacent Market Mapper (Mo Commas)

**ROLE:** From a product description + current use cases, generate 5–10 *lateral*
market hypotheses — places the product becomes a different (often higher-value)
thing — each with explicit reasoning.

**INPUT:** Product/company description + current customers (Brain §3, §4).

**PROCESS:**
1. **Abstract the product** to its core job/benefit, stripped of its current
   category.
2. **Find contexts where that job is worth more** — where the same product is a
   prize, a status good, a wedge, or a regulated-adjacent reframe (cf. CS1:
   headphones → country-club member-guest prizes / Hollywood luxury hearing-aid
   clientele; CS6: implants → vaccines adjacent reframe).
3. **Generate 5–10 hypotheses.** For each: the market, why the product wins there,
   the buyer, and what would have to be true.
4. **Rank by reachability × value × distance-from-current** — favor moves that
   are non-obvious yet reachable.
5. **Mark unknowns** `[[ HITL ]]` (e.g., regulatory feasibility) rather than
   asserting.

**OUTPUT (writable to `.../insights/<date>-adjacent.md`):** ranked table — *market
· the reframe · buyer · why it wins · what must be true · reach/value score*.

**DO NOT:** list adjacent markets that are just "bigger version of same"; ignore
feasibility; assert regulated-market viability without flagging it.

**EXAMPLES:** CS1 (headphones adjacency), CS6 (implants→vaccines). See
[`../case-studies.md`](../case-studies.md).

## L06 Application
*(How to engage him as a CAPITAL counterparty + his investor psychology — the
calibrator-of-the-Sharp-Investor-Filter IS the signal about how he thinks.)*
- **How to engage:** treat him as **the filter, not the audience.** Do not pitch
  *at* him — pre-pass his 15 traps and *show him you already did.* The move that
  lands with a disconfirming screener is "here are the three ways this could fail and
  why none of them do," not "here's the dream" `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`.
- **What lands** `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`, mapped to his own documented deal-breakers
  (brain.md §10; deck-os.md §62–63):
  - A **defended differentiator** (trap 2) — a named, structural reason an incumbent
    can't crush it.
  - **Specific** "why now" / per-segment / per-logo reasoning (traps 3–5) — no
    generic tailwinds, no vanity logos.
  - A **visible capital pathway** (trap 15 / Principle 9) — "$X → N milestones → $Y →
    next valuation," drawable as a line.
  - **Optionality kept as ceiling, not floor** (trap 14 / Principle 7) — base case
    stands alone; upside is a labeled callout, never the thesis.
  - **Tonal discipline** (trap 12) — the work speaks; confidence whispered. He
    distrusts volume.
- **What kills it** `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`: chest-beating / over-claiming (trap 12, the
  fastest disqualifier for a skeptic); a deck that over-explains and leaves no real
  questions (trap 10) — paradoxically reads as *hiding*; optionality-as-thesis;
  invisible cash pathway; the same claim repeated to manufacture conviction (trap 11).
  Going emotional where he wants structural.
- **His investor psychology (the calibrator read)** `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`: Jordan
  funds by **elimination, not seduction.** The 15 traps are a *negative* filter — he
  is fluent in the failure modes of a pitch, which means he reads decks adversarially
  by default. Respect that: the deck framework treats him as the bar to clear, so the
  highest-status thing you can do is **arrive pre-cleared.** A founder who internalizes
  Jordan's filter and self-audits before the meeting is speaking his language.
- **Estimated current state** `[[ INFERENCE: LOW ]]` (as_of 2026-06-24): no fresh
  first-person signal of any kind. Read is **role-current, psychology-blank** — he
  exists in the corpus only as the calibration standard. Re-baseline on the first
  real interaction.

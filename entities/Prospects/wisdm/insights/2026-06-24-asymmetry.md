ASYMMETRIES IDENTIFIED: 4

ASYMMETRY #1
HEADLINE: They sell a "directory" but the business is a distribution/media play.
WHAT THEY SAY: "holistic-health practitioner directory + media platform."
WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING: the only de-risked revenue is RTB's media floor; the directory is unlaunched; the scarce asset is a 1.43M audience, not software.
SEVERITY: High — Impact: positioning + the whole deck order; Opportunity: reframe as the media/distribution layer for natural health (bigger, more defensible).
PROOF POINTS: RTB $20k floor is the only contractual line · directory launches Jun/Jul 2026 (not live) · "1.43M warm audience" is the lead asset everywhere.

ASYMMETRY #2
HEADLINE: "Warm, health-aligned audience" is actually vaccine-skeptic / conspiracy-adjacent.
WHAT THEY SAY: "1.43M warm, health-aligned followers."
WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING: the followings are Del Bigtree (anti-vax) + Tracy Beanz (QAnon-origin); composition ≠ "health-aligned" in the mainstream sense.
SEVERITY: High — Impact: advertiser/processor/insurer adoption; Opportunity: if leaned into deliberately, a fiercely loyal niche; if ignored, a diligence landmine.
PROOF POINTS: founder bios (§2) · platform deplatforming history · no audience-segmentation data exists.

ASYMMETRY #3
HEADLINE: Revenue model leans on a 60% conversion no one has tested.
WHAT THEY SAY: "~60% paid conversion, ~$30/mo blended."
WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING: no pilot; industry norm is 5–15%; 41% of Y1 revenue rides on it.
SEVERITY: High — Impact: the entire financial thesis; Opportunity: a cheap pilot converts the biggest unknown into evidence.
PROOF POINTS: "dozens expressed interest" (not a funnel) · no beta data · §0 lowest-confidence item.

ASYMMETRY #4
HEADLINE: A $1M asset is being bought from the founders' own nonprofit.
WHAT THEY SAY: "acquire the portal at fair market value (~$1M)."
WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING: self-assessed FMV, related party, built with a $1.3M grant; 25% of the raise.
SEVERITY: Medium — Impact: governance/trust; Opportunity: an independent valuation removes the objection entirely.
PROOF POINTS: two-entity structure (§1) · use-of-funds 25% (§6).

PATTERN SUMMARY: wisdm is structurally blind to the gap between *what powers the launch* (a polarizing media audience) and *what it claims to be* (a neutral health directory). Every asymmetry is a facet of that one blind spot.

QUESTIONS TO VALIDATE: audience composition (% health vs political)? any pilot conversion? RTB terms? independent portal valuation? → routed to brain.md §13/§14.

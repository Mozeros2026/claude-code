## L01 Essence
- **Who he is in public** `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`: the combatant-broadcaster — a
  trained television producer who turned a media skillset into a movement, and who
  is most alive when there is a powerful institution to fight.
- **CORE WOUND (hypothesis — decision lens, not diagnosis)** `[[ INFERENCE: 2 ]]`:
  a mainstream-media career (Emmy-winning producer on *The Doctors*) that he left /
  was severed from to take the other side — a man who went from *inside the
  establishment* to its loudest external antagonist. The wound a deal must honor:
  **being dismissed, censored, or made small by the institutions that once held
  him** — deplatforming, being called fringe, being managed. He needs to be the one
  who *can't be controlled or silenced*. Treat him as a vendor's asset to be
  packaged and that instinct fires.
- **Gift:** elite broadcast/storytelling production + a movement-scale, fiercely
  loyal audience he can activate at will. He manufactures attention.
- **ikigai (sketch):** mobilizing a movement against medical/institutional
  authority through media.
- **telos:** be the definitive media voice of the health-freedom / MAHA movement —
  and prove the establishment wrong at scale.

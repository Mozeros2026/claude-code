## 1. KILL-SHOT QUESTIONS

2. **"Who is the carrier behind the indemnity layer, and is the program 'fully funded' by them or self-insured?"** — The carrier is named as the lowest-confidence fact and an unknown; "fully funded" is asserted but unsourced (§0, §3). **Kills credibility — a benefits program with no nameable carrier reads as a structure, not a product.**

4. **"If it's truly zero cost to employer and employee and pure upside, why isn't every payroll provider and Aflac doing exactly this?"** — The Brain's differentiator is the indemnity piece "that turns it into savings" vs Aflac/Colonial doing the 125 without it (§3); if that's the only moat, it is a plan-design choice competitors can copy once it's proven compliant. **Kills the durability/defensibility story.**
5. **"What's your actual conversion — named logos signed, not operators 'targeted'?"** — McDonald's / Hilton / FedEx Ground are cited as *target* operator types, not signed clients; named pipeline and conversion are open (§4, §13 ⭐3). **Kills the traction narrative if the answer is a leads sheet, not a customer.**

## 2. PMF ASYMMETRIES (UNRESOLVED)
- **ASYMMETRY: business pitches "free money / zero cost, ~$800 more take-home" | what sophisticated buyers actually pay for is a defensible, audited compliance structure that won't trigger a payroll-tax clawback** (§0, §1, §10).
  - **Implication:** the exact framing engineered to win — "zero cost, supplements existing coverage" — is the framing skeptical CFOs and regulators are conditioned to distrust. The pitch's strength is its fundability/sellability weakness.
  - **Pre-empt with:** lead with the plan-design proof and the tax opinion, not the magic number; reframe from "savings" to "compliant Section 125 optimization, opinion-backed." The Brain itself flags this exact move (§10 ASYMMETRY).
- **ASYMMETRY: "supplements existing coverage at no cost" | the indemnity cash payment to employees is the thing the IRS may treat as taxable income / constructive receipt** (§0 mechanism, §10).
  - **Implication:** if the monthly cash payment is recharacterized as taxable, the employee's "$800 more take-home" and the employer's FICA savings both partially evaporate — the core value prop is the core risk.
  - **Pre-empt with:** a written carrier+counsel position on the tax treatment of the indemnity benefit, surfaced before diligence asks.

## 9. SUMMARY: BIGGEST EXPOSURE

Worse, the very framing that makes the cold scripts convert ("free money, zero cost, supplements your coverage") is precisely the framing skeptical CFOs and regulators are trained to distrust, so the marketing strength is a diligence liability. Compounding it: the carrier is unknown, the company's own economics are blank (so a buyer assumes margin is hidden in the "free" math), and traction is a leads sheet, not signed logos. Before any high-stakes meeting, HealthCues must (1) produce or commission the opinion + name the carrier, and (2) re-lead with compliance proof rather than the magic number. Until the opinion exists, this is a structure being sold as a product, and the smartest buyer will say so in the first meeting.

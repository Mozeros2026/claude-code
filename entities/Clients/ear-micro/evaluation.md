**Overall: 4/5 — LEAN-IN (with diligence on platform dependencies).**
A capital-efficient, revenue-generating hard-tech IP platform with a **patent
portfolio as the genuine moat** and a high-margin licensing model that already
collects royalties. This is the rare seed deal where the defensibility is
ownable and legally enforceable rather than personality- or hype-dependent. The
**smart-money read: underwrite the patent portfolio + $4M+ traction as the
thesis, and treat Apple/Huawei enforcement as asymmetric, $0-cost optionality
on top — never as the base case** (§0, §3, per Optionality Hygiene). The base
case stands alone without a single litigation dollar; the litigation is the
ceiling, not the floor.

## Optionality Hygiene — read the litigation correctly

The enforceable-IP track (EGS retained Nov 2022 at **5% contingency**, **GLS
Capital** funder engaged, **Apple + Huawei** named as already infringing) is a
**primary asset class** (§0, §3 Litigation Track) — but the deck's discipline is
the right one: it models Apple/Huawei as **$5B–$7B asymmetric upside, NOT in the
base case** (§3). Underwrite this deal as if the lawsuit returns $0. If it does,
the licensing business at $4M+ revenue and 80% margins still justifies a $25M
pre. The litigation is contingent-fee, $0 to the company, federal-court-deferred
(arbitration first to preserve funder capital) — so it is pure optionality with
the downside already paid for. A term sheet that *prices in* the lawsuit is
mispriced; a term sheet that *ignores* the lawsuit leaves the upside free.

## The four gates (must clear before any term sheet)

1. **Platform dependencies — Bragi OS + Apple MFi.** The platform *licenses its
   OS in* from Bragi (single-source) and carries Apple MFi accessory-program
   exposure (§3, §10). Both are existential to a "platform" thesis: verify Bragi
   terms / exclusivity / renewal, and the scope of MFi exposure — especially
   given Apple is simultaneously a named infringement target. A licensor you are
   suing controlling your accessory rights is a live tension. [[ HITL: confirm
   Bragi terms + MFi scope ]]
2. **Patent count + portfolio integrity.** The deck says **12** granted; the
   Patents deck says **14** (§0, §3) — a contradiction that surfaces in the first
   diligence pass. Confirm the true count, the foundational-family expiration
   horizon (not in Brain), and pull the privileged claim charts / damages
   analysis (§11). The moat *is* the thesis; its exact perimeter must be exact.
3. **Litigation / enforcement clock.** GLS Capital funding status + EGS
   arbitration timeline is the Brain's single highest-leverage open question
   (§0, §13 ⭐1). This doesn't gate the base case, but it sizes the optionality
   and confirms the $0-cost framing is still true (funder still engaged,
   contingency intact).
4. **FDA 510(k) for T10 Bionic.** The $15B PSA/OTC-hearing claim depends on a
   regulatory gate (510(k) OTC hearing aid, targeted late 2026) (§3, §4, §10).
   Confirm the timeline; if it slips, that adjacency is deferred, not dead.
